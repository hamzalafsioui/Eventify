The Casablanca Art & Music Festival 2025 celebrates the vibrant spirit of Moroccan creativity, bringing together artists, musicians, and performers from across the globe. Over three exciting days, the city transforms into an open-air gallery filled with live music, visual art exhibitions, and interactive workshops. Visitors can experience the perfect fusion of traditional Moroccan culture and modern artistic expression.


From captivating jazz performances and electronic music sets to local street art displays and culinary experiences, this festival promises something for everyone. Attendees can explore pop-up installations throughout Parc de la Ligue Arabe, meet emerging artists, and enjoy sunset concerts under the palm trees. Join us in Casablanca to celebrate art, diversity, and community in one of North Africa’s most dynamic cultural events.

The Essaouira Waves Sound Festival is where the rhythm of the ocean meets the pulse of music. Held on the beautiful Essaouira Beach, this three-day event brings together top DJs, live bands, and local talents for an unforgettable coastal celebration. The festival blends genres ranging from Afro-house and electronic beats to traditional Gnawa sounds, creating an atmosphere that captures the soul of Morocco’s music scene.


Visitors can dance under the stars, enjoy beach bonfires, and take part in immersive art installations along the shoreline. During the day, attendees can relax with yoga sessions, explore local crafts, and taste fresh seafood from Essaouira’s vibrant food stalls. As the sun sets, the stage lights up with international and Moroccan artists performing against the backdrop of the Atlantic Ocean — making it one of Morocco’s most unique and inspiring festivals.
